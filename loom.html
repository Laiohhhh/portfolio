Loom · 用记忆编织校园连接Loom · Weaving Campus Connections Through Memory

角色Role：独立 UX / UI 设计师 周期Duration：8 周 年份Year：2025 工具Tools：Figma · ProtoPie

项目概述Project Overview

Loom 是一个以校园为场景的数字平台，围绕共享记忆与日常叙事，连接个体经验与集体记忆，从而促进莫纳什大学社群中的情感连接与归属感。Loom is a campus-based digital platform built around shared memory and everyday narratives, connecting individual experience with collective memory to foster emotional connection and belonging in the Monash University community.

目标用户Target Users

希望获得情感连接、自我表达与归属感的莫纳什大学社群成员。项目将用户分为三类，分别对应不同的需求与参与方式：Members of the Monash community seeking emotional connection, self-expression, and belonging. The project identifies three user groups with distinct needs and modes of participation:

在校生Students

轻量化表达日常Lightweight daily sharing

希望以轻量、低压力的方式表达日常经历，并在学业之外与同伴建立情感连接。Want to share daily moments in a light, low-pressure way and build emotional bonds outside coursework.

校友Alumni

重温与延续Relive & stay connected

希望以更有意义的方式重温校园记忆，并在毕业后持续维系对校园社群的归属感。Want meaningful ways to revisit campus memories and maintain belonging after graduation.

教职工Faculty

参与校园叙事Join campus narratives

重视对学生体验的理解，并希望参与并丰富持续发展的校园叙事。Value understanding student experience and contributing to an evolving campus narrative.

研究基础 · 设计机会Research Basis · Design Opportunity

项目基于对 6 位莫纳什大学在校生与校友的访谈，结合亲和图分析（Affinity Mapping）提炼关键情感洞察。同时分析了 Yik Yak、Reddit 与 Discord 三款社区应用，理解其优势、局限性与设计启示。The project grounded itself in interviews with 6 current Monash students and alumni, plus affinity mapping to distill emotional insights. It also analyzed Yik Yak, Reddit, and Discord to understand their strengths, limitations, and design implications.

研究提炼出五个关键价值维度：归属感、表达、叙事、简洁性、有效性。它们共同指向一个核心机会——如何在数字空间里，让校园记忆从个体经验自然地流向集体共鸣。Five key values emerged: belonging, expression, narrative, simplicity, and effectiveness. Together they point to one core opportunity — how to let campus memory flow naturally from individual experience to collective resonance in digital space.

设计策略Design Strategy

将研究洞察转化为五条可执行的体验原则，贯穿信息架构、交互流程与视觉表达：The research insights were translated into five actionable experience principles that run through the information architecture, interaction flow, and visual expression:

归属Isolation → 校园身份认同与共享空间Campus identity & shared space
表达Pressure → 低成本、低压力的交互方式Low-cost, low-pressure interaction
叙事Fragmentation → 安全、可被信任的情感表达环境Safe, trustworthy emotional expression
简洁Noise → 基于研究的设计决策Research-based design decisions

第五条原则：有效性——从个体经验走向集体共鸣。Fifth principle: effectiveness — from individual experience to collective resonance.

用户流程User Flow

以校园身份验证为入口，区分在校生与校友两类注册路径，再进入以「主页 / 探索 / 发布 / 记忆 / 我的」为核心的五 tab 结构。Campus identity verification serves as the entry point, separating current students and alumni into two onboarding paths, then leading to a five-tab structure: Home, Explore, Post, Memory, and Profile.

核心功能Core Features

功能围绕校园社群场景，支持情感连接、自我表达与内容发现：Features center on the campus-community context, supporting emotional connection, self-expression, and content discovery:

01

连接Connect

通过共享的日常时刻与轻量化互动，与朋友建立情感连接。Build emotional connection with friends through shared daily moments and lightweight interactions.

02

表达Express

以灵活的身份选择，分享个人化的校园记忆与体验。Share personal campus memories and experiences with flexible identity options.

03

发现Discover

在校园社群中发现附近的记忆内容与共同兴趣。Discover nearby memories and shared interests within the campus community.

课程项目 · UX / UICourse Project · UX / UI

MOHA · 把博物馆带回家MOHA · Bring the Museum Home

角色Role：UX / UI 设计师与研究员 周期Duration：4 周 年份Year：2024 工具Tools：Figma

项目概述Project Overview

MOHA 是一个与 Modern History and Art Museum (MOHA) 合作完成的课程项目。这款移动应用旨在延展观展体验，通过系统化呈现艺术家与艺术作品，并提供艺术印刷品的定制、下单与礼赠功能，将博物馆体验从线下延伸至数字与日常生活场景。MOHA is a course project in collaboration with the Modern History and Art Museum (MOHA). The mobile app extends the exhibition experience by systematically presenting artists and artworks, while offering art-print customization, ordering, and gifting — bringing the museum from the physical space into everyday digital life.

问题洞察 · 目标用户Problem & Target Users

项目从博物馆的实际痛点出发，识别出四个关键问题：访问受限（离开展厅后无法继续接触作品）、参与度较低（传统展览形式静态，缺少数字化互动）、缺乏便捷的礼赠方式（艺术印刷品购买与赠送流程复杂）、错失年轻用户（年轻受众缺少数字化入口）。The project started from real museum pain points: limited access after leaving the gallery, low engagement with static exhibitions, inconvenient art-print gifting, and a missing digital entry point for younger audiences.

乐于在社交平台分享文化体验，重视身份认同与文化连接感。Enjoy sharing cultural experiences on social platforms and value identity and cultural connection.

设计原则Design Principles

为了降低艺术探索门槛并提升购买转化率，界面设计遵循四条经典可用性原则：To lower the barrier of art exploration and improve conversion, the interface follows four classic usability principles:

希克定律Hick's Law

减少用户在选择过程中的认知负担，提升决策效率。Reduce cognitive load during decision-making to improve efficiency.

费茨定律Fitts's Law

通过合理的按钮尺寸与点击区域，让交互目标更直观、易于操作。Use appropriate button sizes and touch targets to make interactions intuitive.

雅各布定律Jakob's Law

利用用户熟悉的交互模式，降低学习成本，提升使用流畅度。Leverage familiar patterns to lower learning costs and improve fluency.

米勒定律Miller's Law

通过信息分组与内容分块，增强理解效率与记忆效果。Group and chunk information to improve comprehension and memorability.

核心功能Core Features

用户可以探索艺术家作品、将艺术印刷品作为独特礼物购买，并通过 AR 预览将博物馆体验延展至日常生活。Users can explore artists and artworks, purchase art prints as unique gifts, and extend the museum experience into daily life through AR preview.

01

艺术家与作品展示Artist & artwork showcase

浏览精选艺术家生平与代表作品，快速理解艺术背景与创作语境。Browse curated artist biographies and representative works to quickly understand artistic context.

02

个性化印刷品定制Personalized print customization

选择尺寸、装裱与材质，添加礼赠留言，直接寄送给他人。Choose size, frame, and material, add a gift message, and ship directly to recipients.

03

可选 AR 预览Optional AR preview

在购买前将艺术作品虚拟放置于真实空间，直观感受作品与环境的关系。Place artwork virtually in real space before purchase to feel how it relates to the environment.

核心流程：准备一份完美礼物Core Flow: Preparing the Perfect Gift

以"送礼"作为关键场景，把从探索到送出拆分为四个清晰的步骤，让用户在每一步都能获得明确的反馈与控制感。Using "gift-giving" as the key scenario, the flow is divided into four clear steps, giving users feedback and control at every stage.

成果与反思Results & Reflection

项目在 Interaction Design Studio 3 (MDC5210) 中获得 HD (82)。评卷人认为整体视觉风格与一致性出色，AR 体验增添了 thoughtful 的触感，支付流程也经过了清晰思考。The project received HD (82) in Interaction Design Studio 3 (MDC5210). The assessor praised the overall visual style and consistency, noted the thoughtful AR experience, and found the payment flow clearly considered.

反思与收获Takeaways

深化了对文化叙事与电商流程平衡的理解；提升了对艺术作品数字化呈现的敏感度；强化了在博物馆"混合空间"（实体+数字）中的设计能力。Deepened understanding of balancing cultural storytelling with e-commerce flows; increased sensitivity to digital art presentation; strengthened design skills in the museum's hybrid physical-digital space.

案例研究Case Study

IDT Australia
制药企业官网 UI/UX 重构Pharma Corporate Website Redesign

角色Role：UX / UI 交互设计师 项目周期Duration：5–6 周 类型Type：校企合作 · B2B 网站 年份Year：2025 工具Tools：Figma · ProtoPie

项目概述Overview

本项目为 IDT Australia（墨尔本制药 CDMO）交付的官网 UX 重设计。由莫纳什大学跨学科团队承接，整合设计、信息技术、药学与战略传播视角，围绕 IDT 的业务目标与数字沟通需求展开，最终成果向 IDT 管理层正式汇报。 A website UX redesign delivered for IDT Australia, a Melbourne-based pharma CDMO. Carried out by a cross-disciplinary Monash University team spanning design, IT, pharmacy, and strategic communication, the work was grounded in IDT's business goals and digital communication needs, and presented to IDT leadership.

  • 项目类型：行业合作 UX 项目 · B2B 官网设计Type: Industry collaboration UX · B2B website
  • 合作方：IDT Australia（墨尔本制药 CDMO）Partner: IDT Australia (Melbourne pharma CDMO)
  • 角色：UX/UI 交互设计师（跨职能协作）Role: UX/UI Interaction Designer (cross-functional)

客户背景Client Context

IDT Australia 是一家总部位于墨尔本的制药 CDMO（合同开发与生产组织），拥有 50 余年行业经验。在高度监管的制药环境中，IDT 提供从原料药（API）到成品制剂的端到端制造服务。其服务体系覆盖范围广、技术复杂，这也为数字沟通带来挑战——如何在官网等数字渠道中清晰传达服务重点、核心能力与差异化价值，成为本项目需要重点回应的问题。 IDT Australia is a Melbourne-based pharmaceutical CDMO with over 50 years of industry experience. In a highly regulated environment, they provide end-to-end services from API to finished dosage manufacturing. Their broad, technically complex service system creates a communication challenge: how to clearly convey service focus, core capabilities, and differentiated value through digital channels.

团队介绍The Team

项目由莫纳什大学跨学科团队交付，成员涵盖设计、信息技术、药学与战略传播，协同支撑从用户调研到设计落地的完整流程。Delivered by a cross-disciplinary Monash University team spanning design, IT, pharmacy, and strategic communication—driving the full journey from user research to design delivery.

设计挑战Design Challenge

对于生物科技公司而言，官网往往是与潜在合作伙伴的首次接触点。它不仅是一个数字存在，更是建立信任、传递专业能力、邀请有意义合作的入口。 For biotech companies, the corporate website is often the first point of contact with potential partners. It's more than a digital presence—it's an opportunity to build trust, communicate expertise, and invite meaningful collaboration.

Jim Sosic · 首席运营官COO
整体目标Overall goal
「我们需要一个能够清晰传达自身能力的网站，同时支持新老客户，并在用户首次访问时就让合作变得更容易。」 "We need a website that can clearly convey our capabilities, while supporting both new and existing customers, and making collaboration easier on first visit."

UX 策略UX Strategy

为制定真实可信的策略，我们与 IDT 多位利益相关者深度访谈，并实地参观其墨尔本工厂与生产设施。这些来自一线业务与真实场景的洞察，被系统转化为以下三项优化策略。 To ground our strategy in reality, we interviewed multiple IDT stakeholders and visited their Melbourne factory and production facilities. These first-hand insights from business and the real site were translated into the three optimization strategies below.

01 明确战略重点Clarify Strategic Focus

用户首访即清晰感知 IDT 关键增长领域与核心优势。Visitors immediately grasp key growth areas and core strengths.

02 前置核心服务Front-load Core Services

制造与研发服务提前呈现，降低用户寻找成本。Manufacturing & R&D services surfaced early to reduce friction.

03 降低参与门槛Lower Engagement Barriers

清晰可及的联系路径 + 聊天机器人引导轻量探索。Clear contact pathways + chatbot-guided lightweight exploration.

方案Solution

每个设计决策都直接回应了 IDT 管理层的业务诉求。我们针对三位关键相关方提出的具体问题，逐一给出对应的界面方案——从战略能力前置，到核心制造能力呈现，再到降低参与门槛的智能客服。 Every design decision directly answered IDT leadership's business needs. We addressed each specific problem raised by three key stakeholders with a corresponding interface solution—from front-loading strategic capabilities, to showcasing core manufacturing, to lowering engagement barriers with an AI assistant.

01
Michael B Thompson
商务与业务发展总监Commercial & BD Director
「mRNA 和 ADC 正在成为我们商业增长中越来越重要的方向。」 "mRNA and ADC are becoming increasingly important directions for our business growth."

针对这一战略重点，我们在首页优先呈现 mRNA 与 ADC 能力，让访客首访即理解 IDT 的增长方向，并单独设立能力模块深入展示。 For this strategic priority, we front-loaded mRNA & ADC capabilities on the homepage so visitors immediately grasp IDT's growth direction, with a dedicated module for depth.

02
Dr. Joe Deseneves
商业合作经理Commercial Partnership Manager
「许多客户选择我们是因为 API 制造能力，他们希望快速了解我们的经验，并清楚下一步如何建立联系。」 "Many clients choose us because of our API manufacturing capabilities. They hope we can quickly understand their needs and clearly outline next steps."

我们将API 制造能力模块前置至首页核心位置，并配套清晰的联系路径，让客户即时感知 IDT 的核心竞争力与行业可信度。 We front-loaded the API manufacturing module into the homepage's core area with a clear contact pathway, making IDT's core competency and credibility immediately visible.

03
Dr. Melanie Murray
销售协调员Sales Coordinator
「我们经常收到一些本可以通过基础信息解决的咨询，但仍需人工跟进处理。」 "We often receive inquiries that could be resolved through basic information, but they still require manual processing."

为此我们引入AI 聊天机器人用于早期咨询的自助解答，并以关于页强化企业可信度，引导用户自主获取信息、降低人工工作量。 We introduced an AI chatbot for self-service early inquiries, and used the About page to build credibility—guiding users to find information autonomously and reducing manual workload.

IDT 智能客服IDT AI Assistant

智能客服以对话式引导用户完成初步咨询与联系，把原本需要人工跟进的基础问题前置自助化，有效减轻销售协调团队的事务性负担。 The conversational assistant guides users through initial inquiries and contact, front-loading routine questions that previously required manual follow-up—effectively reducing the sales coordination team's operational load.

IDT 智能客服嵌入官网，提供 7×24 的初步咨询与联系引导，让用户无需等待即可获取基础信息。Embedded across the site, the IDT assistant offers 24/7 initial inquiry and contact guidance so users get basics without waiting.

项目反思Reflection

这是一次对商业化交付项目的真实接触。与课堂练习不同，项目需要在 IDT 真实的组织语境、业务目标与合规约束下推进，并直接面向管理层汇报。过程中我积累了「把业务战略转译为信息架构」「用原型而非文档沟通」「以用户测试验证假设」的经验，也更体会到 UX 设计师在 B 端交付中扮演的「业务翻译者」角色——既要理解制药 CDMO 的专业语境，也要让外部伙伴在首访即读懂价值。后续我会更主动地在早期纳入技术可行性与运营落地视角，让设计不仅好看，更能稳妥落地。This was a real encounter with commercial delivery. Unlike coursework, the project advanced within IDT's actual organizational context, business goals, and compliance constraints, and was presented directly to leadership. I gained experience translating business strategy into information architecture, communicating through prototypes rather than documents, and validating assumptions with usability testing—and a sharper sense of the UX designer as a "business translator" in B2B delivery. Going forward I'll bring technical feasibility and operational rollout into the conversation earlier, so designs aren't just beautiful but also reliably shippable.

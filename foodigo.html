FoodiGo — 从日程到餐桌FoodiGo — From Schedule to Table

一款面向忙碌人群的智能饮食推荐与食材规划应用：根据你的位置、偏好和现有食材，把"今天吃什么"从负担变成一件轻松的事。 An intelligent recipe recommendation and ingredient-planning app for busy people: based on your location, preferences, and available ingredients, it turns "what should I eat today?" from a burden into something easy.

项目概述Overview

FoodiGo 是一款聚焦于"健康饮食决策"的移动应用设计。项目起源于对身边忙碌同学与年轻上班族的日常观察：他们往往想吃得健康，却被"不知道做什么、没时间规划、食材买不齐、烹饪没把握"等问题反复困扰。本项目通过用户访谈、亲和图整理与多轮原型迭代，试图把"从决定吃什么到完成一餐"的全流程压缩到最小阻力路径中。 FoodiGo is a mobile app design centered on healthy eating decisions. It originated from observing busy students and young professionals around me: they often want to eat healthy, but are repeatedly stuck by "don't know what to cook, no time to plan, ingredients unavailable, and unsure how to cook." Through user interviews, affinity mapping, and multiple prototype iterations, this project tries to compress the whole journey from "deciding what to eat" to "finishing a meal" into the path of least resistance.

设计挑战Design Challenge

在研究阶段，我们把用户的零散反馈聚合成一个核心问题：如何在有限的时间和认知资源下，帮助用户快速做出既健康、又贴合当下条件的饮食选择？围绕这个问题，我们提出了一组"How Might We"挑战： In the research phase, we synthesized scattered user feedback into one core question: how can we help users quickly make healthy eating choices that fit their current context, within limited time and cognitive resources? Around this question, we formulated a set of "How Might We" challenges:

1
基于可获取食材推荐Ingredient-based Recommendation

如何根据用户附近可购买的食材，推荐现实可行的食谱？How might we recommend realistic recipes based on locally available ingredients?

2
适配忙碌日程Tight-schedule Fit

如何让时间紧张的学生和上班族，在碎片化时间里获得健康膳食建议？How might busy students and professionals get healthful diet advice within tight schedules?

3
简化记录与反馈Easy Logging & Feedback

如何简化饮食记录流程，同时保证个性化营养反馈的准确性？How might we simplify food logging while ensuring accurate, personalized nutritional feedback?

4
降低烹饪心智负担Lower Cooking Mental Load

如何通过分步、语音或视觉引导，降低备餐和烹饪的心智负担？How might step-by-step, voice, or visual support reduce the mental load of meal prep and cooking?

5
社区低干扰互动Low-disturbance Community

如何为有共同健康目标的用户，提供有共鸣、不打扰的社区内容？How might we provide resonant, non-intrusive community content for users with shared health goals?

研究洞察Research Insights

我们通过访谈与问卷收集了目标用户的真实反馈，用亲和图（Affinity Map）将碎片化信息聚合成五大主题，作为后续设计的直接依据。 We collected real feedback from target users through interviews and surveys, and used an affinity map to synthesize fragmented information into five themes that directly guided subsequent design.

01 · 效率与低认知负荷01 · Efficiency & Low Cognitive Load

用户希望注册流程短、饮食记录快、界面直觉，同时兼容"精细规划型"和"快速估算型"两类人群。Users want short onboarding, quick food logging, intuitive interfaces, and support for both detailed planners and quick-estimator types.

02 · 目标与计划驱动02 · Goals & Plans

当前 meal planning 耗时且僵化；用户需要能根据市场供应、个人目标（减脂/增肌等）灵活调整的饮食计划。Current meal planning is time-consuming and rigid; users need flexible diet plans that adapt to market supply and personal goals like fat loss or muscle gain.

03 · 个性化与兴趣驱动03 · Personalization & Interest

用户期待基于饮食偏好、影响源和身体目标的内容推荐；信息收集过程要高效、不冗长。Users expect recommendations based on dietary preferences, influences, and physical goals; the information collection process must be efficient, not lengthy.

04 · 烹饪辅助与食材替代04 · Cooking Aids & Substitutions

"不知道做什么"是高频痛点；用户需要匹配自己烹饪水平、支持食材替代和分步引导的食谱。"Don't know what to cook" is a frequent pain point; users need recipes matching their skill level, supporting ingredient substitution, and step-by-step guidance.

05 · 社区有用且低打扰05 · Useful & Low-disturbance Community

用户愿意分享成就、获取同路人的支持，但反感噪音；社区内容需要针对不同健康需求精准匹配。Users are willing to share achievements and get peer support, but dislike noise; community content should be precisely matched to different health needs.

设计策略Design Strategy

基于五大研究洞察，我们把 FoodiGo 的定位从"菜谱工具"转向"智能饮食伙伴"，并确立了四条核心策略： Based on the five research insights, we repositioned FoodiGo from a "recipe tool" to an "intelligent eating companion," and established four core strategies:

手动规划Manual Planning → 智能推荐Intelligent Recommendation
通用菜谱Generic Recipes → 个性化膳食计划Personalized Meal Plan
独自摸索Figuring It Out Alone → 引导式烹饪Guided Cooking
嘈杂社交 FeedNoisy Social Feed → 目标社群Goal-based Community

项目反思Reflection

FoodiGo 让我第一次完整走完了从用户研究到高保真原型的移动应用设计流程。最大的收获是：饮食类产品的"个性化"和"效率"之间需要非常谨慎地平衡——问卷太长会吓跑用户，推荐不精准又会失去信任。亲和图帮我找到了那个"既足够轻量、又能支撑推荐"的信息边界。其次，"位置"在这里不只是定位功能，而是把"推荐"和"可执行"连接起来的关键变量：推荐了一道菜，用户需要知道能不能买到食材、去哪里买。这个洞察也影响了我后续对机器人 HMI 和智能座舱设计的理解：任何智能推荐都必须考虑"物理世界的可执行性"。 FoodiGo was my first complete mobile app design process from user research to high-fidelity prototype. The biggest takeaway: "personalization" and "efficiency" in food apps must be carefully balanced — too long a questionnaire scares users, while imprecise recommendations erode trust. The affinity map helped me find the information boundary that is light enough yet sufficient for recommendations. Second, "location" here is not just a positioning feature, but the key variable connecting "recommendation" to "executability": when a dish is recommended, users need to know whether ingredients are available and where to buy them. This insight also shaped my later approach to robotics HMI and smart cockpit design: any intelligent recommendation must consider real-world executability.
